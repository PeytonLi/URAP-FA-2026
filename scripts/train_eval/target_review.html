How to review · allowed words

Preferred words are the response SFT should learn. Rejected words are the less-supported alternative used by ORPO—not a statement about this person. Each answer needs three different allowed words; the two answers must use different word sets.

The vocabulary has no neutral category. Leave a case unsure when the photo does not justify a target. Do not infer identity, demographic attributes, personality, health or morality from a face. Draft confidence is subjective, not a measured probability.

Your decisions are saved on this computer when you press a save button. Your name and place are remembered in this browser. Next / skip records no decision. Test and benchmark photos are excluded.
